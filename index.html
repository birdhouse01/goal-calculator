06 · The one number

10

Real conversations a week. That's the whole plan — a $120,000 take-home is 10 two-way conversations about real estate, every week. Everything else on the to-do list is somebody else's emergency.

To get them: 46 contact attempts a week — 10 a day in your morning block, five days a week.

Run lean: 10/wk · Run the MREA team model: 17/wk — leverage costs conversations before it buys you time.

Model presets are the commonly taught shapes — lean solo (~70¢ net per GCI dollar) and the Millionaire Real Estate Agent team economics (~40¢ net). Your own percentages beat both.
A “real conversation” is a two-way exchange about real estate with someone who knows your name.
